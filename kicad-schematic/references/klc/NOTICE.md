## What was copied and changed

The imported content comprises:

- [Symbol graphics and units](graphics.md): S3.1–S3.6 and S3.8.
- [Pins and electrical types](pins.md): S4.1–S4.7.
- [Library fields and power symbols](library-fields.md): G2.1, S5.1–S5.2,
  S6.2 and S7.1.
- The 20 PNG illustrations referenced by these rules, in `images/`.
- The two upstream license files above, copied without modification.

The selected rules retain their wording and IDs. Changes are limited to
selection, conversion of AsciiDoc formatting and image shortcodes into Markdown,
local links between selected rules, and added headings/provenance. Images are
byte-identical to the pinned originals; no cropping, redrawing or recoloring
has been applied. Original image captions remain with the images.

[manifest.json](manifest.json) records each rule's source path and hash, each
image's source and hash, the formatted documents' hashes, and pinned source
links. Source text, PNG metadata and the visible illustrations were checked for
additional notices; none were found for this selection. This is reliance on the upstream license grant,
not an independent determination of every contributor's rights. Unselected
assets may have different notices and require review before copying.

Our [applicability profile](../klc-profile.md), project preferences and exceptions
are maintained separately. They are not upstream KLC rules or endorsed by the
KiCad project. Some upstream screenshots show older KiCad UI or representations;
the profile explains how to apply the rules to a project's target version.
